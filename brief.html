Purpose

Show how Earl thinks and what his taste is, quickly. The site stands in for the portfolio deck (Slides 2 page in the pport Figma file) and should say the same things in a form people can browse and share.

The homepage has one job: make a hiring manager or collaborator want to open the case studies and want to talk.

Audience

  • Design and product leaders hiring for senior design, design management, or head-of-design roles.
  • Peers and collaborators who already know the work and want to point someone at it.
OpenWho matters most? The tone shifts if it is mostly recruiters versus mostly peers.

A1 build notes

  • Portrait: lighter black and white, mixed from the red channel so the red-lit photo doesn't go muddy. A better source photo is still wanted.
  • Ink blob: the pointer pushes the ink away as it passes over the blob or its edges, and it springs back when the pointer leaves. Turned off for reduced motion.
  • Case studies: one page. Clicking Docker swaps the homepage for the case study (link: #docker) with the same header, footer and sheet system. Back returns to Selected work.
  • Docker case study: five sheets from the deck. Stabilize the core, Accounts, billing & AI governance, AI tooling, Docker Hub vision, Making the vision real. Deck images are printed in grayscale and show in color on hover.
  • All case studies (v0.5): Docker (#docker), DigitalOcean (#digitalocean), Facebook (#facebook), Nokia REBEL (#nokia-rebel). Each has a hero, a Challenge/Contribution/Outcome table, an outcome statistics table and a Next link that cycles through all four. Copy is from the deck, including its notes on what was a team or company result.
  • Still needed: a real DigitalOcean image (marked as a stand-in on the page).

  • A report-style data row (report no., subject, sample, most recent). Pink is only a marker underline.

A2 · Press proof

  • The page as a two-plate print proof. A black plate with a pink plate slightly out of register on the big type and headings.
  • Color bars, registration targets and a slug line around the hero. A pink and black duotone portrait.

A3 · Paste-up

  • A photocopied paste-up: the name on taped strips, typewriter slips, and a high-contrast photocopy portrait.
  • Heavier toner grain and a copier-edge shadow. Pink is a highlighter.

Three options (v0.2)

A · Print

From: the Nike Sport Research Lab scan and the Out of Office poster.

  • Gray-cream paper with grain, black ink, and one pink stamp ring. No other color.
  • Typewriter blocks at the top and bottom, like the Nike header. Ink-bleed grotesk with overspray on a few letters, crop marks, an oval mark, "PROD" date and a barcode, like the Out of Office poster.
  • Career shown as a "Summary Career Statistics" table with bars, the way the Nike sheet shows its data. Work shown as dark slide panels.
  • Black-and-white portrait, high contrast, like a scan.

B · Imagine

From: "Let's imagine what's possible" and "Ask us for ideas".

  • Opens on a black sky: ruler grid with tick marks on the left, stars and dust on the right, a centered serif line "Let's imagine what's next." The portrait sits faintly in the stars.
  • Then white with one bright red. Centered serif paragraphs with a few words in grotesk caps, and the employers set in different type styles, like the partner row.
  • Ends with "ASK ME FOR IDEAS" mirrored, full width, bleeding off the bottom. It flips to readable on hover.

C · Index

From: the clean-grid references: the project table site, the Víctor Clemente résumé page, and the label/value layout.

  • Cool gray paper (near-black in dark mode), Source Serif throughout, small mono labels.
  • Large plain paragraphs up top, contact and facts in a side column, a small grayscale portrait in the header.
  • Work as a scannable table (company, work, role, years). Rows open in place, Docker open by default.
  • The grid shows only through alignment and hairlines.

In all three the grid is quieter than in v0.1: faint lines in A, only in the opening sky in B, none drawn in C.

Type

Earl Carlson
Newsreader · weight 200 display, 300 text, italic for emphasis
  • Display and text: Newsreader, optical size on. Light weights at large sizes, 300 for reading. Close to the serif already in the deck.
  • Marks and labels: IBM Plex Mono, small caps-style with wide letter-spacing. Used for the top and bottom strips, dates, and tags.
  • Sizes are fluid (vw-based) for the name and closing line so the bleed holds at any width.

The red comes from the deck and the red-lit portrait. It is the only accent. Hairlines and the grid use the same red at low opacity. Both themes follow the viewer's system setting, with a manual toggle.

Grid and layout

  • 12 columns on desktop, 4 on phones, drawn as faint vertical lines. Content snaps to them, except where the design deliberately breaks out (portrait, surname, closing line).
  • Hero: name, portrait, title. Then the one-line statement and facts, the career timeline, five strengths, selected work, close.
  • The timeline places each employer by year on a 2010–2026 axis. Gaps in the record are shown, not hidden.
  • Top and bottom mono strips frame the page like a printed sheet.
